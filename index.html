About Capacity Connect

A unified digital learning ecosystem

CAPACITY CONNECT is designed to provide a secure and user-friendly learning management environment where trainees can learn, trainers can create and manage educational content, and administrators can monitor the entire platform.


The platform supports professional profiles, course enrollment, learning resources, assessments, certificates, feedback, trainer libraries, announcements and competency-based trainer mapping.
